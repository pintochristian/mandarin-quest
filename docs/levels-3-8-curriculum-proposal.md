# Levels 3–8 Curriculum Proposal (Revised)

Per the explicit instruction not to assume the earlier estimate (~85–90
total lessons) is automatically sufficient for B2, this revision starts
from the target and works backward to a lesson count, rather than the
reverse. It supersedes the Level 3–8 table in
`/Users/christianpinto/.claude/plans/declarative-snacking-music.md`.
**No Level 3 content is authored yet — this is the planning checkpoint
only.**

## 1. Why the original 60–70 lesson estimate was too small

Two independent ways to sanity-check "how much course is needed for B2,"
both pointing the same direction:

**Vocabulary.** CEFR-to-HSK mappings commonly used for Mandarin (Hanban /
Confucius Institute correspondence tables) place B2 around HSK5-equivalent
lexical range — roughly **2,000–2,500 words** of combined active +
recognition vocabulary. Levels 1–2 currently sit at 176 words. Filling the
gap at the ~9 words/lesson pace Level 1–2 actually used would require
**~250 more lessons** on vocabulary alone — nowhere close to 60–70.

**Instructional hours.** FSI category-IV language estimates (Mandarin is
category IV, the hardest tier) put working speaking proficiency
(≈B1/B2) at roughly **1,100–1,300 classroom hours**. Our lessons run
~18–20 minutes; 85–90 lessons is only **~27 hours** of lesson content —
off by more than an order of magnitude if lesson-minutes were the only
source of practice time.

**Conclusion:** lesson content alone cannot and should not try to *be*
1,100+ hours — no self-paced app course does that by lesson count. But
60–70 additional lessons is genuinely too thin even as a scaffold. The
right correction is two-fold: (a) meaningfully increase lesson count and
per-lesson vocabulary load in later levels, and (b) rely on the
now-unlimited **Practice Anytime** system (built this phase) plus SRS
review to supply the repetition volume that lesson content alone can't —
which is the actual pedagogical reason Practice Anytime was worth
building before Level 3 content, not just a nice-to-have.

## 2. Revised target (explicit, falsifiable)

By the end of Level 8:
- **~950–1,050 core active vocabulary words** (formal `VOCAB` knowledge
  nodes, SRS-tracked, drilled directly) — up from 176.
- **~1,100–1,400 additional recognition vocabulary** encountered through
  dialogue and listening content but not independently drilled as
  standalone vocabulary items (matches how real B2 learners actually
  acquire the long tail — through repeated exposure in context, not
  flashcards for every word). Combined lexical exposure: **~2,100–2,450
  words**, in the HSK5/B2-equivalent band.
- **~48–55 grammar/pattern concepts total** (22 already taught + ~26–33
  new), covering the full range spec'd in the original request (comparison,
  aspect markers 了/过/着, 把/被, complement structures, hypothetical and
  causal connectors, register/discourse-level expression in Level 8).
- Course total: **~172–188 lessons** (22 existing + ~150–166 new),
  representing **~58–65 hours of lesson content** — still well short of
  1,100+ hours by itself, but combined with unlimited Practice Anytime
  and ongoing SRS review across the months a real learner takes to work
  through this content, total engaged time plausibly reaches the
  150–300+ hour range, which is a defensible (not overstated) B2 pathway.
  This document does not claim the app alone delivers 1,100 FSI hours —
  no app-only course credibly can — only that lesson content stops being
  the bottleneck once it's this size, and Practice Anytime carries the
  rest, same as spaced repetition apps do for vocabulary-heavy languages.
- Level 8 explicitly targets **register and idiom awareness as a bridge
  toward C1**, not C1 mastery itself — matching "pathway toward C1," not
  a guarantee.

Each level: 4 content modules + 1 cumulative-review module (except Level 8,
which compresses to 3 content modules given its consolidation focus).
Every module ends with a short review lesson; every level ends with a
milestone assessment lesson requiring free production, not just
recognition (addressing remediation-report finding #5).

This is deliberately larger than the original 60–70 estimate (roughly
double), and still described honestly above as a scaffold multiplied by

still reads as too thin once Level 3 is actually authored and tested, the

themes and grammar sequencing below are the part I'd want locked in now.

## 4. Grammar sequencing (builds on existing 22 concepts, no re-teaching)

| Level | New grammar concepts |
|---|---|
| 3 | 会/能 (contrasted with existing 可以); 想/要/应该; 给/跟/离/从/到 prepositions; 先...再...; 一边...一边...; measure-word bridge/expansion (addresses remediation finding #3) |
| 4 | 比/更/最/一样 comparison (with explicit 比 refresher per remediation finding #3); 太...了 (extend existing); 正在/在+verb (extend existing progressive 在); 了/过/已经/还/又/再 |
| 5 | 了 (completed/changed-state) vs 过 (experience) contrast; result/directional complements; 是...的; 因为...所以... / 虽然...但是... |
| 6 | 把; 被 (passive); 得-complements; 应该/必须/得/不得不; 如果...就... / 只要...就... |
| 7 | 如果/要是/即使...也... / 既然...就...; 一方面...另一方面...; multi-clause connected discourse |
| 8 | Consolidation — no major new structures; register/idiom/natural filler vocabulary (就是/其实/反正/怎么说呢/差不多) layered onto existing grammar |

No concept from Level 1–2 is re-taught under a new label (the defect found
in the original outline audit). Every level's first module explicitly
reinforces at least one prior-level grammar concept via the bridge/review
lessons before introducing new ones.

## 5. Vocabulary recycling requirement (enforced, not aspirational)

Applies to all new content, checked mechanically:
- **≥50% of each level's new vocabulary must be lint-verified reused**
  (`lib/content/lint.ts`'s `VOCAB_NEVER_REUSED`) within dialogue in a later
  lesson of the same level — more than double the 38.6%
  (`content/zh/vocabulary-reuse-report.json`) measured in Levels 1–2.
- Every level's first module must deliberately recycle **≥40% of the
  prior level's single-exposure words** (per the remediation report's
  policy recommendation) — `vocabulary-reuse-report.json` is the lookup
  table for this, extended with each level's own output as authoring
  proceeds.
- High-frequency/grammar-load-bearing words (measure words, time
  expressions, location words, the specific words flagged in remediation
  report finding #2: 明天/今天/见面, 左边/右边/一直/远/近/路, 好吃/辣/菜单/服务员,
  家人/同学, 便宜/贵/打折/颜色) are explicitly required reinforcement
  targets in Level 3's first module, not left to chance.

## 6. Exercise variety requirement

Levels 3–8 must not repeat the rigid "exactly 6 exercises, 1 of each type,
same order" template found in 100% of Levels 1–2 lessons
(`RIGID_EXERCISE_TEMPLATE` finding). Exercise count and type mix should
vary per lesson based on content (e.g., a listening-heavy lesson gets 2
LISTENING-family exercises; a grammar-dense lesson gets 2 FILL_BLANK).
Enforced by lint at WARNING severity (does not block import, matches the
"warnings don't block, correctness errors do" rule already implemented).

## 7. Listening, reading, and assessment coverage

- Every level's milestone assessment lesson includes a listening
  comprehension component using only vocabulary/grammar taught up to that
  point (no forward-referencing content).
- Level 5 onward introduces short multi-line listening passages (not just
  single-utterance LISTENING exercises), reflecting the "sequencing/
  storytelling" theme's natural fit for connected speech.
- Pinyin support remains available throughout per existing course design
  (already-fixed `normalizePinyinAnswer` grading applies uniformly to all
  new content — no separate work needed here).

## 8. Rollout process (unchanged from the original plan, reconfirmed)

1. Author Level 3's bridge lesson + Module 1 only.
2. Validate via `lib/content/lint.ts` + Zod, import, manually test via
   preview tools (mobile + desktop), fix issues.
3. Repeat module-by-module through the rest of Level 3.
4. Cumulative check at end of Level 3 (reuse rate via the lint pass,
   grammar progression, listening coverage) before starting Level 4.
5. Repeat per level. Each level is its own checkpoint — this proposal
   does not commit to authoring all six levels in one unreviewed pass.

## 9. What this proposal does not claim

- It does not claim the finished course alone delivers FSI-benchmark
  proficiency hours — no self-paced app course credibly does.
- It does not claim guaranteed fluency outcomes for any individual
  learner — outcomes depend on actual engagement (lesson completion +
  SRS review + Practice Anytime usage), which is outside the course's
  control.
- Level 8 is scoped as a B2→C1 bridge, not C1 mastery.
